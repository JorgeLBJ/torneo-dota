# Feature: tournament-formats
Tournaments get a fixed format chosen at creation: the current "Liga + Playoffs" and a new "Doble eliminación" (6 teams, upper/lower brackets) for the 7 Nov 2026 flash tournament.
Branch: feat/tournament-formats · Delivery: ask-on-risk · Runner: `npx vitest run` + `npm run typecheck` (+ `node --check` for public/*.js)

## Specs
- S1 Several tournament formats (user: "yo digo que el torneo soporte varios formatos de tipos de torneos y el anterior tenga un nombre y este otro"). Format keys `league_playoffs` and `double_elimination`; adding another format later must not need a rewrite. Every existing tournament becomes `league_playoffs` and behaves exactly as today.
- S2 Names and descriptions shown when creating a tournament (user: "que sea intuitivo o deja el nombre y descripcion al momento de generar el torneo"). Approved names: **Liga + Playoffs** ("Todos contra todos con tabla, luego semifinales y final con los 4 mejores") and **Doble eliminación** ("Llaves superior e inferior desde el inicio, posiciones asignadas a mano, gran final BO3"). The format is fixed after creation (shown read-only in Configuración).
- S3 Double elimination starts directly in brackets (user: "que un torneo pueda comenzar directo en brackets con upper y lower"), no fixture and no standings. 6 teams placed by the admin in positions A–F (user's notes: "ya tengo 6 capitanes, y estos por una ruleta en live pickearan a sus 4 integrantes, y a la vez en que posicion estaran: team a,b,c,d,e,f").
- S4 Bracket, as read from the stakeholder's sheet and confirmed: upper U1 A vs B · U2 C vs D · U3 winner U1 vs E · U4 F vs winner U2 · U5 (upper final) winner U3 vs winner U4; lower L1 loser U1 vs loser U4 · L2 loser U2 vs loser U3 · L3 winner L1 vs winner L2 · L4 (lower final) winner L3 vs loser U5; grand final winner U5 vs winner L4 → champion. 10 series.
- S5 Series length (stakeholder notes: "los matchs serán por upper y lower Brackets(bo1), y solo la final bo3"): every bracket series BO1, grand final BO3, no bracket reset (assumption: the notes mention none; whoever wins the grand final BO3 is champion).
- S6 Results drive the bracket: winners advance, losers drop to the lower bracket. Changing or removing a result clears every later series that depended on it. Existing tools keep working for bracket series: games per series, Dota match import, live marking and stream.
- S8 Archive or delete tournaments so they stop appearing (user: "otra cosa adicional que quiero que aproveches es colocar el eliminar torneos o archivarlos y no salgan", screenshot of the admin tournament selector). Archived tournaments disappear from the admin selector and are listed apart in Torneos ("Archivados") with Restaurar and Eliminar. Assumptions: the active tournament cannot be archived or deleted; only an archived tournament can be deleted, after typing its name; deleting removes its data and its stored images (team images, banners); an archived tournament's public link `/t/<slug>` keeps working.
- S7 Public page for a double-elimination tournament shows the upper/lower bracket and the grand final with the champion; tabs that do not apply (Partidos, Posiciones) are hidden. Labels everywhere (admin, captures, live notice, share text, match detail) name the series (e.g. "Final superior", "Gran final").
